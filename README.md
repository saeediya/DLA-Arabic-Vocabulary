# DLA Arabic Vocabulary

DLA stands for دروس اللغة العربية which is a standard textbook of the Arabic language authored by Dr. V. Abdur Rahim.
This page is a collection of Anki decks containing the vocabulary used in these books, created solely for the benefit of Arabic language learners across the world. Hence various decks are available in various languages[^1] so that as many people may benefit from it as possible.

## Modules

Each module/sub-module is an Anki deck/sub-deck containing Anki cards in the predefined [template](https://github.com/saeediya/DLA-Arabic-Vocabulary/edit/main/README.md#template). The modules are arranged according to language of translation. Each language contains two modules (Lang-Ar & Ar-Lang). The modules contain sub-modules corresponding to the level they are based on (Books 1-3).

## Master Vocabulary

The vocabulary is entirely sourced from the official key of the book in the English language, authored by Dr. V. Abdur Rahim himself and published by the Islamic Foundation Trust in Chennai. However, certain changes have been made wherever it has been deemed necessary in order to reduce potential mistakes by students while studying cards made using the type-in card template.

### Vocabulary List

Master pdf files have been prepared containing the vocabulary used in the respective modules. They have been arranged both level-wise (Book 1-3) and lesson-wise (within individual levels). We recommend going through them at least once before studying the cards for the first time. They can also serve as references to go back to whenever needed.
